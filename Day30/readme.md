# Day 30 – Supply Chain Optimizer

## Objective
Built and tested an interactive Supply Chain Optimizer simulator using Claude AI.

## Completed Tasks
- Generated React HTML application
- Tested multiple company profiles
- Selected different supply chain strategies
- Compared business metrics
- Reviewed optimization dashboard
- Analyzed supply chain score

## Key Learnings
- Supplier selection directly affects cost and risk.
- Factory location impacts delivery time and expenses.
- Warehouse strategy influences operational efficiency.
- Transportation choices balance speed and cost.
- Inventory strategy affects customer satisfaction and stock availability.
- Small decisions can significantly change overall supply chain performance.
