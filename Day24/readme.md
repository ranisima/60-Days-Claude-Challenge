# Day 24 – Business Strategy Report

## Step 1: Read the Resources
- Open the Day 24 resources from your course portal.
- Read all the provided materials carefully.

## Step 2: Watch the Solution Video
- Watch the complete Day 24 solution video.
- Note any important instructions or tips.

## Step 3: Open Claude
- Go to Claude AI.
- Sign in to your account.

## Step 4: Set Claude Effort Level
- Set the effort level to **Low**.

## Step 5: Continue Your Day 23 Conversation
- Open the same Claude conversation used for Day 23.
- Do **not** start a new chat.

## Step 6: Provide Your Day 23 Customer & MVP Blueprint
- Copy your Day 23 Customer & MVP Blueprint.
- Paste it into the existing Claude conversation.

## Step 7: Paste the Business Strategy Prompt
- Copy the Business Strategy prompt from the Day 24 resources.
- Paste it into Claude.

## Step 8: Generate the Business Strategy Report
- Ask Claude to generate the complete Business Strategy Report.
- Wait until the report is fully generated.

## Step 9: Review the Business Reality Check
Review:
- Market assumptions
- Risks
- Validation suggestions
- Business feasibility

## Step 10: Analyze the Business Model Canvas
Review:
- Customer Segments
- Value Proposition
- Channels
- Customer Relationships
- Revenue Streams
- Key Resources
- Key Activities
- Key Partners
- Cost Structure

## Step 11: Review Revenue & Pricing Strategy
Check:
- Pricing model
- Revenue streams
- Profit potential
- Monetization strategy

## Step 12: Study the Go-To-Market Strategy
Understand:
- Product launch plan
- Marketing channels
- Sales strategy
- Customer journey

## Step 13: Analyze Customer Acquisition Strategy
Review:
- Organic growth
- Paid marketing
- Referral programs
- Partnerships
- Customer retention

## Step 14: Review the First 100 Users Plan
Understand how to acquire:
- First 10 users
- First 50 users
- First 100 users

## Step 15: Study Competitive Position & Moat
Review:
- Competitors
- Competitive advantage
- Unique selling proposition (USP)
- Long-term moat

## Step 16: Analyze Reverse SWOT Analysis
Review:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Threats
- Reverse SWOT insights

## Step 17: Review Investor One-Liner & Founder Pitch
Read:
- Investor one-liner
- Founder elevator pitch

## Step 18: Review Investment Scorecard
Analyze:
- Market score
- Product score
- Team score
- Financial score
- Overall investment rating

## Step 19: Study the Visual Dashboard
Review:
- Revenue projections
- Market opportunity
- Growth metrics
- Business health indicators

## Step 20: Analyze the Final Verdict
Review:
- Go / No-Go recommendation
- Key findings
- Next steps
- Improvement suggestions

## Step 21: Export or Save the Report
Save the report as:
- `Business_Strategy_Report.pdf`
- or `Business_Strategy_Report.md`
