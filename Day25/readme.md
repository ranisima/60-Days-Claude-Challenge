# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Step 1: Read the Resources
- Open your course portal.
- Go to **Day 25**.
- Read all the provided resources carefully.

---

## Step 2: Watch the Solution Video
- Watch the complete Day 25 solution video.
- Note any important instructions.

---

## Step 3: Open Claude
- Visit Claude in your web browser.
- Sign in to your account.

---

## Step 4: Set Claude Effort Level to Low
- Start a **New Chat**.
- Set the **Effort Level** to **Low** (if available).

---

## Step 5: Start a New Conversation
- Click **New Chat**.
- Do not continue an old conversation.

---

## Step 6: Paste the AI Shark Tank Simulator Prompt
- Copy the **AI Shark Tank Simulator** prompt from your course resources.
- Paste it into Claude.
- Press **Enter**.

---

## Step 7: Generate the HTML Application
- Wait for Claude to generate the complete HTML application.
- Ensure it includes:
  - Startup Input Form
  - AI Investor Panel
  - Question & Answer Interface
  - Startup Scorecard
  - Investment Decision
  - Leaderboard
  - Downloadable Pitch Report

If Claude stops generating the code, type:

> Continue from where you stopped. Output only the remaining HTML code.

---

## Step 8: Save the HTML File

Save the generated code as:

```text
AI_Shark_Tank_Simulator.html
```

- Save as type: **All Files**
- Encoding: **UTF-8**

---

## Step 9: Open the Application
- Double-click the HTML file.
- Open it in your preferred web browser.

---

## Step 10: Enter a Startup Idea

Example:

```text
EcoCart
```

or

```text
AI Marketplace for Local Businesses
```

---

## Step 11: Complete All Startup Input Fields

Fill in all required details, such as:

- Startup Name
- Problem
- Solution
- Target Customers
- Revenue Model
- Market Size
- Competition
- Founder Experience
- Funding Required
- Business Model

---

## Step 12: Start the Shark Tank Simulation

- Click **Start Simulation**.
- Wait for the AI investors to begin asking questions.

---

## Step 13: Review Questions from Each AI Judge

Typical questions may include:

- Why now?
- What problem are you solving?
- What makes your startup unique?
- How will you acquire customers?
- What is your revenue model?
- Why should investors invest?

---

## Step 14: Answer Investor Questions

Provide clear and thoughtful responses.

Example:

**Question:**

> Why is your startup different?

**Answer:**

> Our AI platform reduces operational costs by automating supplier selection and comparing prices in real time.

---

## Step 15: Analyze the Startup Scorecard

Review metrics such as:

- Innovation
- Market Opportunity
- Business Model
- Revenue Potential
- Scalability
- Team Strength
- Execution
- Overall Score

---

## Step 16: Review the Investment Decision

Check whether the AI investors recommend:

- Invest
- Pass
- Conditional Investment

Read the reasoning behind the decision.

---

## Step 17: Analyze Valuation & Funding Recommendations

Review:

- Suggested Valuation
- Funding Recommendation
- Equity Percentage
- Growth Strategy
- Key Risks
- Next Milestones

---

## Step 18: Test the Leaderboard

- Run another simulation.
- Confirm that the leaderboard updates correctly.

---

## Step 19: Download the Pitch Report

- Click **Download Report** or **Download Pitch Report**.
- Save the generated report.

---

## Step 20: Take Screenshots

Capture screenshots of:

1. Home Screen
2. Startup Input Form
3. AI Judges Asking Questions
4. Startup Scorecard
5. Investment Decision
6. Leaderboard
7. Downloaded Report

---

## Step 21: If Claude Reaches Usage Limits

Wait until your usage limit resets.

Then continue with:

> Continue generating the remaining HTML from where you stopped.

---

day25.md

Place the following files inside the **Day25** folder:
