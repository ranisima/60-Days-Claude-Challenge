    Welcome to your Personal AI Playbook

    What this is

    This is a personal toolkit of reusable AI prompt systems, built around how you actually work: reviewing code, debugging, and writing code with Claude. Instead of one-off prompts you write from scratch each time, you get ready-made templates you fill in and reuse — plus tools to build your own.

    Dashboard & Workflows

    Dashboard gives you a quick overview — favorites, recent activity, categories. Workflows is your full library: ready-made prompt templates grouped into Code Review, Debugging, and Writing Code. Open one, fill in the blanks, and copy it straight into Claude.

    Prompt Builder & Loop Builder

    Prompt Builder lets you assemble a custom prompt from building blocks like Role, Objective, and Tone, with a live preview as you go. Loop Builder takes any task and wraps it in a self-checking loop — the AI attempts, evaluates itself against your criteria, and improves, until a stop condition you set is met.
