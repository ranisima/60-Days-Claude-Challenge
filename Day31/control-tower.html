Shift Briefing

You are the new Head of Operations. For the next 3 minutes, disruptions will hit every part of the network — ports, suppliers, trucks, factories, warehouses. Every call moves the numbers. Keep the network standing.

  • 01 Alerts arrive continuously and stack up. Pick which one to solve first — the clock is part of the decision.
  • 02 Each alert offers a strong response, a weaker one, an ignore, and a delay. Choose fast, choose well.
  • 03 Unresolved alerts expire on their own and hurt the KPIs just like ignoring them.
  • 04 Difficulty ramps up — more alerts, less time to react, as the shift goes on.

How This Works

A quick reference for running the tower.

  • KPI Service Level, Customer Satisfaction, Inventory Health and Transport Efficiency are healthiest near 100%. Operating Cost is healthiest when low.
  • BEST The green action is the strongest fix for that alert — biggest KPI gain, protects the most revenue.
  • OK The blue action resolves the alert but with mixed trade-offs.
  • DELAY Buys a little time now, but usually costs KPIs a few seconds later — use sparingly.
  • IGNORE Drops the alert immediately with the worst outcome. Letting the timer expire does the same thing automatically.
  • GRADE Your final grade (A+ to D) is based on total score, KPI health, and how many alerts you resolved well.
